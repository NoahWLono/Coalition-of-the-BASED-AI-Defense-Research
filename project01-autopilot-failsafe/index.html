A saved experiment comparing ArduCopter 4.5.6 and 4.5.7 under twelve simulated fault scenarios. Explore original outcomes, observed mode transitions, and the evidence behind each result.

Simulation evidence onlyThese are real executions of software-in-the-loop (SITL) with a synthetic aircraft and fictional site. They do not establish operational flight safety. LAND denotes a telemetry mode transition; this viewer does not run a simulator.

Public evidence copies: host identifiers and local paths have been replaced with fictional values. Telemetry checksums and publication seals were refreshed; recorded flight observations were compared before and after redaction. Read the redaction record and verification results.
