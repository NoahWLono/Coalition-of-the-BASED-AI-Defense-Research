All inputs and annotations are AI-generated. This experiment uses no patient data, geographic routes or real delivery observations. It has no operational or clinical validation.

Tiny-case evidence

A bounded synthetic study

One controlled benchmark stream, a five-minute grid and simplified batteries. No energy physics, supply-handling rules or aviation requirements are validated. Online sensitivity and unexpected weather were not evaluated.

Verification scope

Numerical solver bounds apply to the finite integer-slot model. Rolling MILP uses only requests revealed by each decision; local bounds provide no global online optimality gap or competitive ratio. All code, annotations and review were produced by AI assistants.
