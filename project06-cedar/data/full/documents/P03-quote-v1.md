# Birch Inspect Standard — commercial quotation

SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION MATERIAL. Document P03-quote-v1. Issued 2026-10-01.

This is a fictional commercial document for Birch Inspect Standard, prepared for the invented Cedar Civil Inspection Service. All organizations, product claims, laboratory results, prices, and administrative events in this document are synthetic. The package is limited to unarmed civilian infrastructure inspection and environmental surveying. It contains no routes, targets, or connection details for aircraft.

This quotation separates aircraft prices from shared equipment and recurring charges. Amounts are in Canadian dollars and exclude taxes. The quotation covers six aircraft, one shared ground station, initial training, and the listed charger and interface kit. Shipping within the fictional service region is included; no currency conversion or discount rate is implied.

Annual software and support fees begin at fleet acceptance. Only those recurring fees receive the quoted annual escalation. Replacement battery price is per unit; one replacement per aircraft per year is the buyer's planning assumption, not a supplier prediction of battery life. The optional carrying-case upgrade can be removed without changing the mandatory package.

Payment is assumed to be thirty percent at order acceptance and the balance after a documented delivery inspection. The dates and payment terms are illustrative and create no binding offer. A future purchaser would need to settle acceptance criteria, tax treatment, cancellation rights, and any excluded interfaces in a real agreement.
