# Fenland Service Thermal — product specification and synthetic laboratory attachment

SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION MATERIAL. Document P12-spec-v1. Issued 2026-10-01.

This is a fictional commercial document for Fenland Service Thermal, prepared for the invented Cedar Civil Inspection Service. All organizations, product claims, laboratory results, prices, and administrative events in this document are synthetic. The package is limited to unarmed civilian infrastructure inspection and environmental surveying. It contains no routes, targets, or connection details for aircraft.

The proposed fleet uses a common ground-station application so that the buyer can train staff on one review workflow. The inspection payload records material for later human assessment. Image interpretation, permission to operate, and acceptance of survey findings remain buyer responsibilities. The quantities below describe the offered package; accessory availability and delivery promises are addressed in the separate quotation and terms.

Endurance is a nominal claim under an invented still-air fixture with a fresh battery. It is unsuitable for planning a real flight. Payload allowance is a procurement comparison field rather than evidence that any particular sensor is integrated. Environmental limits, operator decisions, and local conditions would require separate validation before operational use.

The attached fictional North Quay Laboratory memorandum NQL-26-P12 describes twenty repeated readings at an invented stationary reference mark, a stated synthetic spread, and no field trial. The response marked independent_test represents this supplied fictional report. It does not mean a laboratory exists, a physical test occurred, or an actual product capability was established.
