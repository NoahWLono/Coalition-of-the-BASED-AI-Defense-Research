SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION MATERIAL. Document P05-terms-v1. Issued 2026-10-01.

This is a fictional commercial document for Cobalt Terrain Base, prepared for the invented Cedar Civil Inspection Service. All organizations, product claims, laboratory results, prices, and administrative events in this document are synthetic. The package is limited to unarmed civilian infrastructure inspection and environmental surveying. It contains no routes, targets, or connection details for aircraft.

Support includes the documented ground-station release branch, routine maintenance guidance, and a contact channel for reproducible defects. The committed period starts at buyer acceptance. Renewal prices beyond that period are not promised by the current offer. The recurring price in the quotation must therefore be read alongside the term stated here; projecting a fee does not extend the contractual commitment.

Delivery requires buyer approval of the final bill of materials and receipt of the initial payment. An estimate in an informal conversation would not replace this written commitment. Training covers lawful civilian survey work, routine care, and transfer of inspection files. Attendance does not certify an operator or authorize flight.

Warranty remedy is repair or replacement of an accepted defective item during the stated warranty period in the full schedule. Consumables and accidental damage are excluded. Service targets are procurement terms for comparison and would require a negotiated acceptance process in a real purchase.

The synthetic report-cache component at version 1.0.0 is present and the supplier declares that its optional import path is enabled. The fictional issue is therefore marked affected for this fixture, with a commitment to discuss the fixed 1.1.0 release. No real vulnerability or exploit is asserted.
